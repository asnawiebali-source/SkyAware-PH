Weather & Disaster Information Portal

About SkyAware PH

Empowering communities across the Philippines with accessible weather information, natural event updates, and disaster preparedness resources — all in one platform.

Our Mission

To provide accessible and understandable environmental information that helps Filipinos make informed decisions and prepare for potential natural hazards.

Our Vision

A more informed and disaster-ready Philippines, where communities can easily access useful weather information, hazard updates, and general safety resources.

What SkyAware PH Offers

SkyAware PH brings different information sources together so users can access environmental data and preparedness tools in one place.

Weather Forecasts

View current temperature, humidity, wind speed, weather conditions, and seven-day forecasts for Philippine cities.

Air Quality Monitoring

Check air quality information such as the US Air Quality Index, PM2.5, and PM10 measurements.

Earthquake Updates

Explore recently reported earthquakes, including magnitude, location, depth, and event time.

Natural Events

Browse available NASA EONET records about significant storms, wildfires, volcanic activity, and other natural events.

AI Safety Assistant

Ask questions and receive general disaster preparedness guidance using an AI-powered assistant.

Saved Locations

Save preferred cities for convenient access and manage them using add, view, edit, and delete features.

Our Five API Integrations

SkyAware PH combines five external service providers to support its weather, disaster-information, and location features.

01

Open-Meteo

Weather forecasts and air quality information.

02

USGS

Recent earthquake reports and seismic event data.

03

Groq AI

AI-powered answers to disaster safety questions.

04

NASA EONET

Curated natural-event information from NASA.

05

Nominatim

Philippine place search and geographic coordinates.

Our Objectives

SkyAware PH aims to make useful environmental information easier to find and understand.

Improve access to information Organize weather, earthquake, air quality, and natural-event information in one platform.
Support disaster preparedness Provide general safety resources that encourage awareness and informed decision-making.
Provide a user-friendly experience Present environmental information through clear dashboards, location searches, and responsive pages.
Important Safety Reminder

SkyAware PH is an informational platform and does not replace official government warnings, emergency services, or professional safety advice. Information from external APIs may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. During emergencies, always follow PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, and your local authorities.
