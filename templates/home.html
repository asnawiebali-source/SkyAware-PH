About SkyAware PH

SkyAware PH is a web-based weather and disaster information portal designed to help Philippine residents access weather forecasts, earthquake information, air quality updates, and emergency preparedness resources in one convenient platform.

Our goal is to promote awareness, preparedness, and informed decision-making through accessible information and modern technology.

SkyAware PH provides informational data from external services. Always follow official government advisories during emergencies.
