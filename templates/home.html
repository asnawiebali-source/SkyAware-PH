ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Preparedness starts with information.

SkyAware PH is a weather and disaster information portal designed to help communities in the Philippines access forecasts, earthquake information, air quality updates, and safety resources.

Our goal is to make essential information easier to access and encourage informed decision-making during changing weather and disaster conditions.

Important: SkyAware PH provides information from external services. During emergencies, always follow official government advisories and local authorities.
