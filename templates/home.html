About SkyAware PH

SkyAware PH is a weather and disaster information portal designed to help residents in the Philippines access useful environmental information and disaster preparedness resources.

The platform combines information from external services to provide weather forecasts, air quality data, earthquake reports, natural event records, and AI-assisted safety guidance.

Its goal is to make important information easier to access and understand, helping users stay informed and better prepared for potential hazards.

Important Safety Reminder: SkyAware PH provides information from external sources and general disaster preparedness guidance. It does not replace official emergency alerts. Always follow local authorities, PAGASA, and PHIVOLCS.
